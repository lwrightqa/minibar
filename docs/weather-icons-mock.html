Weather icon indicators

Three ways to show the state with an icon instead of a scene. Low Glare Pixel colours, Handjet type, 640 × 172. Icons are PixelLab art at 32 × 32, shown at 2×. Sample values: Newton, 18°, high 22°, low 12°.

Units

1 · Icon beside the temperature

2 · Large icon in the top-right corner

3 · Icon in the info column, on the left

4 · Icon as a full-height column, text stacked on the right

5 · Small icon top-left, big temperature bottom-left, highs and lows right-aligned top-right

6 · Everything centred on one vertical axis

7 · Right-aligned text block, small icon at mid-left, highs and lows on one line

8 · Oversized icon bleeding off the right edge, temperature large top-left
