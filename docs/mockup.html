How you'd control it

Touch
Tap cycles status, a swipe steps back or forward, and holding opens a quick menu with brightness, Wi-Fi, calendar and battery. On the Pomodoro screen, a tap starts or pauses the timer and holding opens timer options.
Pomodoro
25-minute focus sessions, 5-minute breaks and a 15-minute break after every fourth session, all adjustable. Focus shows in orange so people know not to interrupt; breaks turn the bar teal or blue to say it's a good moment. The timer keeps running when you switch to another status, with a small countdown in the corner, and the bar jumps back to it when a phase ends.
Side buttons
BOOT jumps to the next status without looking. PWR turns the screen off and on, and holding it powers down.
Phone remote
The device serves a small page at tinybar.local on your Wi-Fi, like the Remote panel on this page.
Automations
An HTTP endpoint (and optionally MQTT for Home Assistant) so a calendar, Slack, Zoom or a shortcut can set the status.
Calendar
Optionally reads a private calendar link and switches to "In a meeting" on its own.
Tilt
The motion sensor flips the layout when you mount it upside down, so the buttons can go on either side.
Chime
The speaker plays a short chime when a focus session or break ends. The mock-up plays it in your browser.

Hardware it uses

Display
3.49" IPS, 172 × 640, AXS15231B over QSPI
Touch
AXS15231B over I²C
MCU
ESP32-S3R8, 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash
Clock
PCF85063 RTC, synced over NTP
Motion
QMI8658 6-axis IMU
Audio
ES8311 codec + speaker
Power
USB-C or 3.7 V Li-ion
