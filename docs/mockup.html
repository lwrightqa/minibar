How you'd control it

Touch
Tap cycles status, a swipe steps back or forward, and holding opens a quick menu with brightness, Wi-Fi, calendar and battery. On the Pomodoro screen, a tap starts or pauses the timer and holding opens timer options. A dark screen wakes on the first tap, and every action shows a short confirmation.
Pomodoro
25-minute focus sessions, 5-minute breaks and a 15-minute break after every fourth session, all adjustable. Focus shows in orange so people know not to interrupt; breaks turn the bar teal or blue to say it's a good moment. Each finished session earns a red tomato, upcoming sessions show as faded tomatoes, and the one in progress fills with red from the bottom up as you focus. The timer keeps running when you switch to another status, with a small countdown in the corner, and the bar jumps back to it when a phase ends.
Side buttons
BOOT jumps to the next status without looking. A press of PWR turns the screen off and on, holding it for 3 seconds powers the bar off, and a press turns it back on. If the screen is dark or an alarm is ringing, the first press of either button only wakes the screen or silences the alarm.
Power
On battery, the software is what keeps the bar switched on, so it also handles switching off: hold PWR for 3 seconds, or use Power in the quick menu, which also has Restart. A watchdog restarts the bar if the software ever freezes, so it can't get stuck on, and it shuts itself down before the battery runs flat. On USB the bar always has power, so powering off puts it into a deep sleep until you press PWR. While it's off, the Remote page can't reach it and says so.
Phone remote
The device serves a small page at tinybar.local on your Wi-Fi, like the Remote panel on this page.
Automations
An HTTP endpoint (and optionally MQTT for Home Assistant) so a calendar, Slack, Zoom or a shortcut can set the status.
Calendar
Optionally reads a private calendar link and switches to "In a meeting" on its own.
Flip
Turning the bar over always does three things: the layout turns the right way up, any alarm goes quiet, and the Pomodoro starts whatever it's waiting for (the next session or break, or a paused timer). A timer that's already running is left alone, so a flip never skips anything.
Alarm
When a session or break ends, the bar flashes and chimes every few seconds until you tap, press a button or flip it, and gives up after a minute. With auto-start on, it chimes once and carries on. The mock-up plays the chime in your browser.

Hardware it uses

Display
3.49" IPS, 172 × 640, AXS15231B over QSPI
Touch
AXS15231B over I²C
MCU
ESP32-S3R8, 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash
Clock
PCF85063 RTC, synced over NTP
Motion
QMI8658 6-axis IMU
Audio
ES8311 codec + speaker
Power
USB-C, or 3.7 V Li-ion on MX1.25 (pin 2 +), charges at 1.2 A
