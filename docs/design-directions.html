TinyBar · Design directions

Four looks for the bar

The same three moments in each direction: Busy, a Pomodoro focus session, and On a call (the new automatic status from the Mac app). Screens are drawn at the device's real 640 × 172 proportions with each direction's own fonts. Pick one, or mix parts of several. Live versions you can apply to the simulator are coming to the mock-up.

Directions by the team's four UX designers. Every font is a Google Font under the SIL Open Font License, and every text color was checked against its background for at least 4.5:1 contrast (WCAG AA). Sample times, durations and app names are examples.
