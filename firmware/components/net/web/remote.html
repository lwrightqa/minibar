Where do I find it?
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar. The phone app doesn't have this setting.
  2. Click the gear, then Settings.
  3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click your calendar.
  4. Click Integrate calendar.
  5. Under "Secret address in iCal format", click the copy button, then paste the address here.

Google Calendar › Settings › your calendar › Integrate calendar › "Secret address in iCal format". Keep it private: anyone with it can read your calendar. TinyBar only reads it and never shows it again after you save it. If the setting is missing, your work account may have it turned off; ask IT.

Off: the bar and this page show meeting times only. Everyone near your desk can read titles, and events marked Private never show one.

Connect your Mac

The TinyBar menu-bar app sets On a call whenever your Mac's mic or camera is in use, so Slack huddles and Google Meet calls show up even when they aren't on your calendar. It talks to TinyBar over USB when the bar is powered from your Mac, otherwise over Wi-Fi.

To use it over Wi-Fi, choose Pair with a code in the Mac app. This TinyBar shows the code.
