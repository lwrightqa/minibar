Display
Time format

12-hour shows 3:30 PM. 24-hour shows 15:30, with no AM or PM. It applies to the clock, meeting times, Next up and Away, here and on the bar.

Clock color

The Clock screen takes this color on the bar. Pomodoro shows as its focus orange. The other statuses keep their own colors.

Theme

Bold Signal fills the screen with the status color. Low Glare Pixel is dark, with the color in the words and a thin edge, so it gives off less light.

A soft chime and one flash when a calendar meeting starts. It's quiet during calls and other meetings, and while you're Away, and then it only flashes. Off turns both off.

Automatic status

Your calendar and your Mac can set the status for you, separately or together. Whatever you pick yourself wins until the call or meeting ends.

CalendarsNot set up

    Add a calendar

    The tag (up to 4 letters or digits) shows next to Next up on the bar once you have 2 or more calendars. Everyone near your desk can read it, so don't put anything private in the name or tag.

    Where do I find it?
    1. On a computer, open Google Calendar. The phone app doesn't have this setting.
    2. Click the gear, then Settings.
    3. On the left, under Settings for my calendars, click the calendar you want to add.
    4. Click Integrate calendar.
    5. Under "Secret address in iCal format", click the copy button, then paste the address here.

    Google Calendar › Settings › the calendar › Integrate calendar › "Secret address in iCal format". Any calendar that gives you a secret iCal address works. Keep it private: anyone with it can read that calendar. MiniBar only reads it and never shows it again after you save it. If the setting is missing, your work account may have it turned off; ask IT.

    You can add up to 3 calendars. Remove one to add another.

    Off: the bar and this page show meeting times only. Everyone near your desk can read titles, and events marked Private never show one.

    Meetings from all your calendars are merged, soonest first, and the bar shows one Next up. A meeting that's in two of your calendars shows once. A calendar that can't sync is left out of the bar until it works.

    Connect your Mac

    The MiniBar menu-bar app sets On a call whenever your Mac's mic or camera is in use, so Slack huddles and Google Meet calls show up even when they aren't on your calendar. It talks to MiniBar over USB when the bar is powered from your Mac, otherwise over Wi-Fi.

    To pair your Mac, plug this MiniBar into it once. Or, in the MiniBar menu on your Mac, choose Connect…, then Pair Over Wi-Fi. This MiniBar shows the code.

    Jira
    Issue countNot set up

    MiniBar asks your Jira site how many issues are in one filter, every 5 minutes, and shows the number as its own screen you can swipe to. It only reads. Until you save this, the screen stays out of the swipe order.

    Only sent to your own Jira site, to sign in. MiniBar never sends it anywhere else.

    Write-only: once saved, MiniBar never shows it again. Create one in your Atlassian account (Security, API tokens, Create API token). A token can do anything your account can, so keep it private; Remove here erases it from MiniBar.

    The number after filter= in the filter's address. You can paste the whole address.

    The label shows on the bar, so everyone near your desk can read it. Above the alert number the screen turns orange and says so.

    Set a goal to track progress: reach a target count, or reduce by a number. Shows on the Jira screen.

    Counts are approximate by design. The bar asks over HTTPS and checks Jira's certificate; it never follows a redirect with your sign-in.
